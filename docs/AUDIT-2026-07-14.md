## The one-paragraph version

The site's problem was never the design, it was the map and the missing instrument. Eight flat tabs made four of them (Solutions, Programs, Platform, Innovation Lab) compete for the same slot in a visitor's head, and there was nothing between "read the site" and "book a call" for the majority who are not ready to talk. Both are fixed: six top-level items with a Solutions menu, and a Tools tab holding a real, sourced, interactive Spend Diagnostic that turns a cold visitor's own stack into the exact problem Ayvede sells the fix for. Nothing was deleted, no copy on existing pages changed, no token touched.

## Ranked findings

### Fix first (done in this build)

1. **Navigation taxed every visitor.** Solutions vs Programs vs Platform vs Innovation Lab is Ayvede's org chart, not a visitor's mental model. A cold visitor had to understand the business before they could navigate it. Now: Home, Solutions (Advisory, Programs, Platform, Innovation Lab), Tools, Insights, Vision, Connect. Same visual language, one level of depth, zero content lost.
2. **No middle rung on the conversion ladder.** Every path ended at "book a call," which only converts the already-convinced. Competitors run interactive quantifiers as their conversion engine (altari.ai's revenue-leak calculator sits mid-page, right before the CTA). Ayvede now has the governance version, and it is better: real list prices, named sources, honest uncertainty flags.
3. **Vision sat in slot two.** The manifesto came before the offer. Vision now sits after Insights; the founder story is still one click away everywhere.

### High impact (recommended next, needs your call - none applied, all touch content)

4. **Footer social labels are fake links.** LinkedIn, X, YouTube, Instagram render with a pointer cursor and do nothing. To a careful buyer that reads as broken. Link them or remove them until the profiles exist. Two-minute fix once you decide.
5. **"100% Client Satisfaction" is the weakest KPI on the strip.** Round-number vanity metrics get discounted by exactly the buyers you want. Consider something verifiable: years in practice, industries served, jurisdictions covered.
6. **No named social proof anywhere.** Simplexico's entire conversion engine is ten named testimonials (Linklaters, Bird & Bird). Even two anonymized-but-specific quotes ("Managing partner, 40-attorney New Jersey firm") would outperform most of the page copy.
7. **Booking is mailto-only.** A scheduling link on Connect (Calendly or similar, booking with you by name) cuts friction to one click. Every strong peer does this. Keep the mailto as the fallback.
8. **Nobody in this market shows pricing.** Not altari, not Simplexico, not BABL. Publishing even the Diagnostic's fixed fee would be a category-level trust signal and is exactly the Hormozi move: anchor the entry offer, let the ladder do the rest.
9. **Productize the first call.** altari's single best mechanic: the free call produces three named take-home deliverables in 48 hours, "yours regardless." An Ayvede "Governance Snapshot" (usage map, ranked gaps, one-page roadmap) would do the same for advisory.
10. **Name the obligations.** BABL converts by naming frameworks in its nav (EU AI Act, ISO 42001, NIST AI RMF). Compliance buyers search by obligation. Working state bar guidance, SEC and FINRA expectations, and NIST AI RMF into Advisory and Programs copy would catch that traffic.

### Cut or resist

11. **Home duplicates Solutions and Programs** (six service cards, eight program chips). Preserved per the guardrail, but if you ever want a tighter home page, that is where the knife goes.
12. **Six top-level items is the ceiling.** Future diagnostics go inside the Tools page registry, never into the nav.
13. **Resist compliance badges without evidence.** altari's unbacked SOC 2 and ISO logos read as distrust to a high-trust audience. Your current approach (named deliverables, NDA-first, documented-by-design) is the credible version.

Weaknesses: zero pricing, proof counters render "$0 saved" without JavaScript, hollow badges, no governance language at all. - **Ayvede's position after this build**: the only one in the set with an honest, sourced, interactive diagnostic and a governance-first voice. The open flank nobody occupies is pricing transparency (finding 8).

## What changed in the code (and nothing else)

- **Navigation**: NAV data now supports groups. Desktop dropdown matches the nav language exactly (JetBrains Mono uppercase, gold active underline, dark panel, chevron that rotates). Opens on hover or click, closes on selection, outside click, or Escape, with aria attributes. Mobile burger shows a SOLUTIONS group label with indented subtabs. Footer now lists all nine destinations.
- **Tools tab (new page)**: The Spend Diagnostic. 38-tool catalog across seven categories at real July 14, 2026 list prices, each row sourced during research; rates that could not be confirmed on a vendor page are marked "rate unverified" in the UI. Seat steppers, dollar inputs for usage-based APIs, add-your-own rows. Live monthly and annual totals, tools and seats count, a spend-versus-stranded bar at the sourced 36 percent benchmark (Zylo 2026 SaaS Management Index), and suite-aware overlap flags across twelve capability groups (selecting Microsoft 365 plus Zoom plus Otter correctly flags meetings, chat, and transcription overlap, because Teams, OneDrive, Gemini, and AI Companion are modeled as suite inclusions). A benchmarks section cites four stats with named sources. A registry block lists two future diagnostics as "In the Lab." CTAs: Book a Scoping Call (lands on Connect with "AI & SaaS Spend Diagnostic" preselected) and Send My Results to Ayvede (opens a prefilled email to you carrying their itemized stack, totals, and flags).
- **Home**: one slim card after The Problem section pointing to the diagnostic. The only content addition to an existing page.
- **Connect**: one new option in the interest dropdown; the form accepts a preset.
- **Pricing data is an editable array** at the top of the source with an as-of date and per-row notes, so updating prices is editing rows and rebuilding, nothing structural.
- **Untouched**: every existing page's copy, all tokens and fonts, the Insights hub (all ten briefings, filter, search, reader), both existing forms, phone, gmail address, copyright 2026, the Wix shell, domain and DNS.
